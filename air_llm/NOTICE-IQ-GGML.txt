IQ CODECS — UPSTREAM PROVENANCE

The IQ2_S, IQ3_XXS and IQ3_S layouts, dequantization definitions and immutable
codebooks derive from ggml-org/llama.cpp commit
7df8b876b91208a86a6d792e1140b3deec14535b:
  ggml/include/ggml.h
  ggml/src/ggml-common.h
  ggml/src/ggml-quants.c

https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/7df8b876b91208a86a6d792e1140b3deec14535b

The production implementation is an original bounded NumPy translation of the
referenced C functions. Codebook byte order follows the little-endian GGUF
layout. The 11,400-byte payload includes three grids, ksigns_iq2xs and
kmask_iq2xs. NumPy views are read-only; reader budgets reserve 16 KiB for their
payload and view headers before lazy import. The reservation is conservative
per reader, even when readers share the same process-global codebooks.
